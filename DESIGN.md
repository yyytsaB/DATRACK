# DATRACK design system
## Direction
Dark mint, modern native Android. Operational clarity and meaningful detail take priority over decorative effects. Soft charcoal surfaces with a restrained mint accent and warm white type.
## Typography
Android sans at Material type roles. Body 14–16sp, readable secondary text, large balance at 36sp. Text wraps; actions never compete with promo names. No glow, gradients or all-caps section eyebrows.
## Layout
20dp page gutter, 24dp section spacing, 12dp grouped spacing, 16dp surface radius. Navigation bar keeps five destinations and text labels. Screen headers carry context and one explanatory line.
## States and interaction
48dp touch targets. Save/error/deletion/permission recovery preserved. Promo actions occupy their own row. Forecast and widget distinguish expired, depleted, learning and stale states. Main balance always includes a unit. Charts remain interactive and horizontally scrollable.
## Verification scope
Samsung A22 5G, native renders at standard and enlarged font settings. Preview package only.
