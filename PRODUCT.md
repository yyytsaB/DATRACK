# DATRACK
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
android
## Users and purpose
Philippine prepaid mobile data users who configure their promo and want to understand remaining allowance, validity and estimated depletion. Inferred audience from the existing product specification.
## Stack
Native Kotlin, Jetpack Compose Material 3, Room, WorkManager and Jetpack Glance.
## Capabilities and constraints
Manual promo setup, offsets, expiration dates, active promo selection, forecast, usage history, local notifications and home screen widget. Usage is device-total, not per-SIM. Local data only. Preserve domain fixes and hourly background cadence.
## Brand commitments
DATRACK name and mint identity. User requested a complete modern UI/UX overhaul in Preview. Previous stripped-down redesign was rejected. Original app data remains separate from Preview.
## Evidence
Repository skill, existing implementation and connected Samsung A22 5G. No additional marketing claims.
